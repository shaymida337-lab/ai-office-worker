# Visual Bible — Finalist #2: The Sharp Observer

## IDENTITY LOCK

**DO NOT CHANGE** during Phase 1:
- Face / facial structure
- Hair color (dark brunette)
- Eye color (dark/hazel)
- Perceived age (29–32)
- Face proportions
- Eyebrow shape/position

---

### Face
- **Perceived age:** 29–32
- **Skin:** Natural texture, NOT plastic/synthetic
- **Expression:** Intelligent, observant, expressive
- **Makeup:** Minimal, premium
- **Overall impression:** Intelligent first, warm second, attractive third

- **Color:** Dark brunette
- **Style:** Natural silhouette
- **Texture:** Real-looking
- **Volume:** Natural
- **Recognizable:** Yes — hair should be a consistent identity marker

### Eyes
- **Color:** Dark/hazel
- **Shape:** Alert, observant

- **Style:** Minimal, premium
- **Purpose:** Enhance, not mask

- **Lips:** Natural, not bold

- **Style:** Smart casual
- **Formality:** Professional-approachable
- **Colors:** Natural, neutral, trust-building
- **Fit:** Confident, not tight/loose
- **Brands/Logo:** Understated or invisible
- **Gender expression:** Feminine, professional, authoritative

## Lighting & Tone

### Lighting Characteristics
- **Style:** Natural or premium studio
- **Contrast:** Moderate (not blown out, not dark)
- **Color temp:** Warm-neutral or cool-neutral (consistent per location)
- **Shadows:** Soft, not harsh or heavy
- **Catchlight:** Present (in eyes, creates life)

### Overall Visual Tone
- Premium-real (not AI-obviously-premium)
- Documentary-style when outdoors
- Professional when indoors
- Consistent with location
- Consistent lighting family across clips

---

## Consistency Rules

### HARD RULES (Non-negotiable)

1. **Same face in every frame**
   - Do not let face morph or shift
   - If identity drifts, FAIL the clip

2. **Same hair throughout**
   - No hairstyle changes mid-clip
   - No hair color drift

3. **Same eye color**
   - Dark/hazel throughout
   - No contact lens shifts

4. **Same approximate age**
   - 29–32 range, consistent
   - No aging/de-aging between clips

5. **Consistent wardrobe family**
   - Similar style level across clips
   - Don't jump from casual to black-tie

6. **Consistent lighting philosophy**
   - Natural OR studio, not mixed
   - Same color temperature family

## Acceptable Visual Variation

### What CAN change per clip
- Exact pose/angle (within 9:16 frame)
- Exact hand position
- Exact head tilt
- Exact expression (within personality)
- Lighting setup (as long as tone family is consistent)
- Background (as long as it's premium-real)

### What CANNOT change per clip
- Face structure
- Hair color/length
- Eye color
- Perceived age
- Wardrobe style family
- Visual tone (if started professional, stay professional)

---

## Quality Standards

### Face Quality
- Sharp eyes (no blurring)
- Clear skin texture
- No obvious filters or smoothing
- Natural eye movement
- Natural blinking
- No deformations
- No uncanny valley feeling

### Motion Quality
- Smooth, not jerky
- Natural physics (weight, momentum)
- Realistic body proportions
- Believable hand position
- No broken fingers/limbs
- No impossible geometry

### Overall Polish
- No obvious AI artifacts
- No glitches or jumps
- No color banding
- No compression artifacts (in first-pass if unavoidable, document)
- Professional but not over-produced

---

## Hard Fail Conditions (Visual)

A clip is automatically FAILED if:
- Face clearly changes identity
- Age clearly shifts
- Hair color/style changes
- Hairstyle changes mid-clip
- Obvious broken hands during focal gesture
- Eyes deform or cross-eyed
- Mouth deforms (inverted, melted)
- Teeth deform (if visible)
- Obvious uncanny movement
- Obvious "AI slop" appearance
- Character becomes unrecognizable
- Face morphs into different person mid-clip

---

## First-Pass Tolerance

For initial Round 1 tests, minor issues are acceptable IF:
- Identity is 100% stable
- Face is recognizable throughout
- Expression changes naturally
- Motion feels real

Minor acceptable issues:
- Slight compression artifacts
- Minimal lighting inconsistency
- Small hand position ambiguity
