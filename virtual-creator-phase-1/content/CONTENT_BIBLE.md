# Content Bible — Finalist #2: The Sharp Observer

**STATUS:** Concept phase (do NOT produce content yet)

Content creation will begin after motion and voice are validated.

---

## Core Content Promise

Every strong video should ideally make the viewer think:

**"I never noticed that."**

This is the primary success metric for content resonance.

---

## Content Pillars (Research Phase Only)

### DISCOVER (40% of mix)
**Goal:** Reach new viewers
**Typical format:** Finding a business mistake or pattern
**Length:** 15–90 seconds
**Examples to research:** Codie Sanchez, Barbara Corcoran observation content

### PROVE (25% of mix)
**Goal:** Build credibility
**Typical format:** Testing, showing evidence, explaining methodology
**Length:** 60–180 seconds
**Examples to research:** Marie Forleo teaching methodology

### BOND (20% of mix)
**Goal:** Build relationship
**Typical format:** Personality, behind-the-scenes, interaction
**Length:** 15–60 seconds
**Examples to research:** Jasmine Star personal content

### REMEMBER (15% of mix)
**Goal:** Build character/IP recognition
**Typical format:** Recurring segments, signature behaviors, memorable moments
**Length:** 15–45 seconds
**Examples to research:** Barbara Corcoran catchphrases and signature bits

## Recurring Show Concepts (Future — Do NOT Build Yet)

### 1. FOUND IT
**Format:** Discovery of a business mistake or pattern
**Typical length:** 30–90 seconds
**Tone:** Detective mode
**Key element:** Moment of discovery, highlighting the insight
**Promise:** "You won't see this the same way again"

### 2. FIX THIS
**Format:** Problem identification → solution
**Typical length:** 60–120 seconds
**Tone:** Friend mode with authority
**Key element:** Simple, actionable fix
**Promise:** "This is easier than you think"

### 3. BUSINESS COURT
**Format:** Two opposing viewpoints → evidence → verdict
**Typical length:** 90–180 seconds
**Tone:** Authority mode
**Key element:** Data, reasoning, fair judgment
**Promise:** "Here's who's actually right"

### 4. I TESTED IT
**Format:** Real experiment or test → results → learning
**Typical length:** 90–180 seconds
**Tone:** Authority mode with discovery
**Key element:** Methodology, real data, surprising (or expected) results
**Promise:** "This is what actually happened"

### 5. COFFEE
**Format:** Personality and relationship content
**Typical length:** 15–45 seconds
**Tone:** Friend mode
**Key element:** Personal insight, behind-the-scenes, relatable moment
**Promise:** "Get to know me"

### 6. HUMAN vs AI
**Format:** When AI is useful vs when a human is better
**Typical length:** 60–120 seconds
**Tone:** Authority mode with nuance
**Key element:** Clear comparison, honest assessment
**Promise:** "Here's the real trade-off"

## Content Hook Mechanisms (Research Only)

**Analyze but do NOT copy verbatim:**
- How Barbara Corcoran hooks attention (first 2 seconds)
- How Codie Sanchez structures discovery (setup → revelation)
- How Lori Greiner uses direct language (call to action)
- How Jasmine Star builds personality (authentic sharing)
- How Marie Forleo teaches (story → principle → action)
- How Sara Blakely uses humor (dry wit, relatable problems)

**Extract reusable mechanisms:**
- Hook structure (not exact wording)
- Pacing (not timing)
- Storytelling (not scripts)
- Conflict arc (not situations)
- Payoff structure (not specific joke/insight)
- Comment triggers (what invites conversation)
- Series logic (how to link videos)
- Retention mechanics (what makes people keep watching)

## First 100 Posts Strategy (Future)

Do NOT pre-produce 100 posts.

**Research and strategy only:**
- Posts 1–20: Find hooks (what catches attention)
- Posts 21–40: Find formats (which formats work best)
- Posts 41–60: Find character attachment (what makes audience like HER)
- Posts 61–80: Find repeatability (which shows/formats can sustain)
- Posts 81–100: Find IP recognition (signature elements, catchphrases, visual identity)

**Launch in controlled batches, not all at once.**

## Content Topics (Phase 1 Safe Zones)

Safe to create content about:
- Business systems and processes
- Customer service failures and fixes
- Workflow inefficiencies
- Tool evaluations and comparisons
- Business psychology (not personal psychology)
- Small business observations
- Market trends and patterns
- Decision-making frameworks
- Measurement and metrics

---

## Content Topics (Phase 1 RESTRICTED)

Do NOT create content about:
- Mental health or mental health advice
- Medical advice or health claims
- Body image or appearance standards
- Parenting or child-rearing
- Deep relationship advice
- Trauma or personal struggles
- Political opinions
- Moral judgments on personal choices
- Lived experience narratives (she has none)

## QA Checklist for All Content (Future)

Before publishing any video:
1. ✅ Identity is stable (same woman throughout)
2. ✅ Claims are backed by evidence or clearly marked as opinion
3. ✅ No fabricated biography or lived experience
4. ✅ Tone matches personality (not preachy, not arrogant)
5. ✅ Audio/visual quality meets standards
6. ✅ Captions are accurate (if included)
7. ✅ No sensitive domain violations
8. ✅ Founder has approved final cut

## Success Metrics (Future)

- Views per video (reach)
- Shares per video (resonance)
- Comments per video (engagement quality)
- "I never noticed that" mentions in comments (core metric)
- Audience retention by second (pacing fit)
