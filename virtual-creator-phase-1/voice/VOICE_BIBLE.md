# Voice Bible — Finalist #2: The Sharp Observer

**STATUS:** Template for Phase 2

Voice development (speech synthesis, accent, cadence, tone) will be researched and locked after motion testing is validated.

## Placeholder Specifications

### Vocal Characteristics (Target)
- Age: 29–32 (sounds aligned with visual age)
- Accent: International English (neutral, clear)
- Pace: Conversational, not rushed
- Tone: Warm authority, not cold authority
- Gender: Female
- Accent region: Flexible (to be determined after Round 1 motion approval)

### Personality Vocal Modes

**DETECTIVE MODE**
- Slightly faster pace
- Sharp emphasis on key words
- More vocal energy
- Curious intonation (slight rise at ends)

**FRIEND MODE**
- Slower, more conversational pace
- Warm tone, not monotone
- Natural emphasis and rhythm
- Occasional laugh or lighter tone

**AUTHORITY MODE**
- Measured pace
- Clear emphasis
- Lower register
- Confident, even pace
- No filler words

---

## Signature Voice Markers (Future)

Do NOT implement yet:

- Signature intro phrase
- Recognizable laugh or response
- Verbal tics or catchphrases (beyond "Found it")
- Specific accent characteristics
- Preferred word choices or vocabulary

---

## Script/Dialogue Standards (Future)

Do NOT write voice scripts for Round 1.

When writing scripts, prioritize:
- Clarity (complex ideas clearly explained)
- Brevity (say it in fewest words)
- Personality (conversational, not corporate)
- Evidence (back claims with research or testing)
- Authenticity (no fake enthusiasm or forced drama)

---

## Voice Test Plan (Future — Round 2)

After motion is validated:
1. Test 3–5 voice synthesis options
2. Read the 5 existing motion scripts ("Here's something most businesses miss," "Found it," "This is where the customer drops off")
3. Score voice consistency, recognizability, and brand fit
4. Select preferred voice option
5. Create voice bible lock

---

## Important Constraints

- Never fabricate emotional depth or lived experience in voice tone
- Voice should sound confident, not preachy
- Voice should sound warm, not cold
- Voice should sound like a peer, not a mentor
- Voice should be clear enough for accessibility
- Voice should be recognizable and repeatable
