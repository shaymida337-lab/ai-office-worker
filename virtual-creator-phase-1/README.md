### Success Criteria

**PASS if:**
- 5/5 clips generated
- 4–5 clips score ≥7/10 (weighted)
- Face identity is stable in all clips
- No hard fail conditions present
- Cost stays under $10 budget

**PROCEED to Phase 2 if:** 5/5 clips pass

**RERUN if:** 4/5 clips pass (rerun the failed one)

**CHANGE STRATEGY if:** 3/5 or fewer clips pass

**REJECT if:** Identity is unstable across clips

---

## Critical Rules (Non-Negotiable)

### Identity Stability
- **Same face in every frame.** If face morphs or changes, clip FAILS.
- No identity drift is acceptable.
- This is the foundation of the entire project.

### Motion Quality
- Smooth, natural motion (not jerky or AI-slop)
- Realistic physics (weight, momentum)
- Natural expressions and gestures
- No broken limbs or impossible geometry

### Trust
- No fabricated biography or lived experience
- Clear distinction between opinion, observation, research, experiment
- No false claims or unverified statements
- AI nature is transparent
