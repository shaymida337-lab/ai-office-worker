- Minimal premium makeup
- Smart-casual wardrobe
- Premium-real environments: office, café, small business, street
- Avoid luxury-flex, nightclub, supercar, neon-tech, oversexualized or generic “AI influencer” styling

If identity drifts, do not “fix it with editing.” Mark the clip as failed and record why.
